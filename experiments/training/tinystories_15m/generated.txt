Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he saw a big box in his room. It was a small box. Tim wanted to open it.
Tim asked his mom, "Can I open the box?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim was very excited. He wanted to see what was inside. He asked his mom, "Can I touch the box?" His mom said, "No, Tim. It is not a toy. It is a toy."
Tim was sad, but he knew he had to be careful. He took the toy and started to clean it. He was very happy. He played with his toy all day long.
